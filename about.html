Our Mission

The mission of biashara os is to make businesses easy to manage for the small and medium scale business who work with us.
By giving them this platform we aim to make record-keeping and financial managment easy and simple for these operators.

Our Vision

To enable every small and medium scale business have great financial and stock managment.

    Steps to use our application
  1. Sign in with email
  2. Register your business name and type
  3. Provide a suitable password
  4. Create a list of all the stocks with their amounts and other iformation
  5. Connect your mobile wallet with the application
  6. Provide different types of regular expenses in your business
  7. Choose the theam of your licking
  8. Start officialy with our application
