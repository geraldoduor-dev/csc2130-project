Biashara OS
Biashara OS is a small busines financial and  recorrds management platform.
it helps to simplify record-keeping in small businesses and eliminates paper trails that cause confusion.

TARGET USERS
1.Kiosks
2.Mama mbogas
3.Small hotels
4.Small amd medium shopes


CORE FEATURES:
1.Record sales(eventualy link to m-pesa )
2.Record expenses and categorise them
3.View current cash position
4.Track customers and suppliers
5.Record debts and credits
6.Generate daily,weekly and monthly report
7.Manage stock - record goods received,damaged, and supplier details

WHY CHOOSE It
I saw the local shopes in the univerity had now effective way of record keeping,they were either using books,exel spreadsheets or had no
record keeping at all.
This caused loses,time wastage and was nota  good business structure.
The existing platforms are made for larger business and the small ones are left out.
